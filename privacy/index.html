Merge Battles Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Merge Battles collects, why, who else receives it, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

Who we are

Merge Battles is made by Dor Tal, trading as Juice Gaming, the developer shown on Google Play. Dor Tal is the controller of your data and decides how it is used. Contact: info@juice-gaming.com.

What we collect and why

You do not need to sign up. The first time you open the game, it creates a guest account for you. You can later link a Google account if you want to.

DataWhy we use it
Player ID: a random account ID created by the app To identify your account, keep your progress and run your matches
Device ID: a random ID the app creates on your device. It is not your Android ID or advertising ID To sign your device in to your account, and to slow down attempts to break into accounts
Game data: your matches and their results, the player IDs of your opponents, stars, level, streaks, coins, lives, achievements and game settings To run the game, keep your progress, match you with other players, and detect cheating
Game events, such as "match finished", linked to your player ID To understand how the game is played, balance it, fix problems and compare game settings (see Testing game settings)
Server logs: IP address, device and app type, player ID, the time and the request made To keep the service running and secure, prevent abuse and fix errors
Google account, only if you sign in with Google: your Google account ID and the email address Google sign-in provides To let you keep your account across devices, recover it, and to confirm who is asking when you contact us about it
Purchase records, only if you make a purchase: what you bought, when, the price, and the order reference from Google Play To deliver what you bought, restore it, handle refunds, prevent fraud, keep the records tax and accounting law requires, and measure which game settings work best (see Testing game settings)

We do not collect your name, phone number, postal address, location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera. We never see your payment card details: Google Play handles payment. We collect your email address only if you sign in with Google or email us.

The game has no ads, does not use the advertising ID, and has no chat.

Testing game settings

We may give different groups of players different game settings, such as prices, rewards or board layouts, and compare how each group plays and buys (sometimes called A/B testing). This uses your player ID, your game data and events, and your purchase records. It does not use the advertising ID, and the results are used only by us to improve the game.

Legal basis

Where the GDPR or a similar law applies, we use your data because we need it to provide the game you chose to play (performance of a contract), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the game secure, preventing cheating and fraud, fixing it and improving it, including by testing game settings. We keep purchase records because the law requires it (legal obligation).

Who else receives data

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Only these receive it:

  • Service providers who host and run our servers and store data for us. They process data only on our instructions and may not use it for their own purposes:
    • Amazon Web Services runs our servers and databases and stores their backups, in its Stockholm region (Sweden, EU).
    • Grafana Labs (Grafana Cloud) receives our server logs and performance measurements so we can find faults and keep the game running, in its Sweden region (EU). The logs it receives include IP addresses and player IDs.
  • Google, through Google Play services: Google Play distributes the game and handles purchases (Google Play Billing), and Google sign-in signs you in if you choose it. What Google collects is covered by Google's privacy policy.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires us to.

During a match, your opponent's app receives your player ID as part of the match data. The game does not display it.

Our servers and the data they hold are in the European Union (Sweden). Some of our service providers are companies based outside your country. Where the law requires it, we use safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for those transfers.

Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). The secret that signs your device in is stored on our servers only as a one-way hash. Access to our systems is limited and logged.

How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is deleted within 30 days, except what is listed below.
  • A closed-account marker: your Player ID (a random ID the app created) and the date the account was closed are kept, with nothing else linked to them, so that the same Player ID can never be used again.
  • The deletion record: your Player ID, the dates, and which of our systems confirmed the deletion, kept for 90 days after the deletion is complete, to show that it was done.
  • Inactive guest accounts: a guest account (not linked to Google) that has not signed in for 12 months is deleted automatically, with its game data.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days, then deleted. The copy our monitoring provider (Grafana Labs) holds is deleted after 14 days.
  • Backups: copies of our databases are kept for 30 days and then deleted. Data you delete also disappears from the backups when they expire, so at most 30 days after it was deleted from the game.
  • Security records, such as records of administrative actions taken on an account: up to 12 months, to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Bans: if an account linked to Google was banned, a one-way hash of the Google account ID and the reason for the ban are kept for 2 years after the account is deleted, so that a ban cannot be escaped by deleting the account. Nothing is kept for accounts that were not banned.
  • Purchase records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires (up to 7 years), even after you delete your account. They are then no longer linked to a game account.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and game data at any time. How depends on how you play:

  • Guest players: in the game, open Settings → Delete account.
  • Players signed in with Google: in the game, open Settings → Delete account. If you no longer have the game, the account deletion page explains how.

We do not delete an account because of an email alone, because a Player ID is not secret and does not prove the account is yours. Deletion cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account. Full details of what is deleted and what is kept are on the account deletion page.

Children

Merge Battles is meant for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, email info@juice-gaming.com. We will look into it and close the account when we can confirm it. Because a Player ID is not secret, we do not delete an account only because an email names it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or Israel), you may also have the right to object to or restrict our use of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to your data protection authority. To delete your account, use Settings → Delete account in the game (see Deleting your account). For any other request, email info@juice-gaming.com. Because a Player ID is not secret, we ask you to confirm in the game that the account is yours before we give out, change or delete its data. We answer within 30 days.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no scripts, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the game.
