Delete your Merge Battles account

This page is for the app Merge Battles on Google Play, by the developer Juice Gaming.

You can ask for your Merge Battles account and its data to be deleted at any time. How you do it depends on whether you play as a guest or signed in with Google.

If you signed in with Google

  1. Open Merge Battles. If you no longer have it, install it again and choose Continue with Google with the same Google account. Your account comes back.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is closed straight away.

Deleting your account on this page with Sign in with Google, without the game, is coming. Until it is available you can also email info@juice-gaming.com with the subject "Delete my Merge Battles account". We reply with the steps above.

Why we do not delete on an email alone: a Player ID is not secret, so an email quoting one does not prove the account is yours. Confirming in the game does, so nobody else can delete your account.

If you play as a guest

  1. Open Merge Battles.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account. This screen also shows your Player ID.
  3. Confirm. Your account is closed straight away.

Uninstalling the game does not delete your account.

Inactive guest accounts

A guest account that has not signed in for 12 months is deleted automatically, with all of its data. Accounts linked to Google are not deleted for inactivity.

What is deleted

Within 30 days of your request, we delete:

  • your account: device IDs, sign-in sessions and, if you linked one, your Google account ID and email address (your Player ID is kept as a closed-account marker, see below);
  • your progress: stars, level, streaks, achievements and game settings;
  • your wallet: coins, lives and their history;
  • your match history and the game events linked to your account.

Deletion cannot be undone. Anything you bought in the game is lost with the account.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • A closed-account marker: your Player ID (a random ID the app created) and the date the account was closed are kept, with nothing else linked to them, so that the same Player ID can never be used again.
  • The deletion record: your Player ID, the dates, and which of our systems confirmed the deletion, kept for 90 days after the deletion is complete, to show that it was done.
  • Purchase records (what was bought, when, the price and the Google Play order reference): kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, up to 7 years, no longer linked to a game account.
  • Server logs (IP address, device and app type, Player ID, time and request): kept for 30 days from when they were written, then deleted.
  • Security records, such as records of administrative actions taken on an account: kept for up to 12 months, to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Bans: if an account linked to Google was banned, we keep a one-way hash of the Google account ID (it cannot be turned back into the ID or an email address) and the reason for the ban for 2 years, so that a ban cannot be escaped by deleting the account. Nothing is kept for accounts that were not banned.
