Merge Battles

A fast head-to-head merge puzzle game. Two players, two boards, one race to the hidden treasures.

When it is out, you will find it on Google Play from the developer Juice Gaming.

  • Head to head Two boards side by side, and one winner.
  • Merge to get ahead Merge your pieces to reach the hidden treasures first.
  • Attacks Spend energy on attacks that slow your opponent down.

About us

Why “juice”?

Game designers call it juice: the pop, the bounce and the little burst of feedback that makes a move feel good. We care about it in every tap. That is the name, and the glass in our logo.

How we work

We are a small independent studio. Merge Battles has no ads, and we do not sell your data. Our privacy policy says what the game collects, and you can delete your account whenever you want.
